# DA40 XLS (N523LT) Checklists

Offline checklist viewer for iPad (also iPhone and desktop) for N523LT, a 2008 Diamond DA40 XLS with Garmin G1000 / GFC 700 and PowerFlow exhaust. Built from the PA-34 Seneca app (github.com/douglasbrody/pa34-checklists, v70 / checklists v42). Big tiles, text that resizes to fit the screen, Day / Night / Red modes.

**Status (v1):** engine and flight-data features working; checklist content, weight & balance figures and performance data are **placeholders until the DA40 XLS AFM and N523LT's signed W&B record are uploaded**. Preflight Planning lists what is missing and calculates nothing until then. No figure in this app comes from memory or from another airplane.

**Install:** open https://douglasbrody.github.io/da40-checklists/ in Safari on the iPad → Share → **Add to Home Screen** → open it once from the icon while online. After that it works in airplane mode (Edit shows "offline ready"). The icon is green and labelled DA40 (the Seneca icon is blue).

**Sharing the origin with the Seneca app:** both apps live under douglasbrody.github.io and share one localStorage and one cache storage. Every DA40 storage key is prefixed `da40.` (`da40.checklists.v1`, `da40.flightdata.v1`, `da40.plan.cfg.v1`, `da40.theme.v1`, …), and the service-worker cache is `da40-checklists-v<N>`; its activate handler deletes only caches starting with `da40-checklists-`. The Seneca app's `sw.js` needs the matching fix (delete only `checklists-` caches): as published (v70) it deletes every other cache on the origin, including this app's offline copy, each time the Seneca app updates.

**Aircraft data:** everything aircraft-specific is in `aircraft.js` with its AFM section/page in `src`: empty weight and arm (W&B record), stations and arms, combined limits, fuel (usable, lb/gal, arm, taxi allowance), MTOW / MLW / MZFW (a null limit is skipped), the CG envelope as a polygon, cruise fuel flows by power, maximum demonstrated crosswind (kt), the time meters, and which AFM chart or table answers each performance question. Home base KBDR: elevation 9 ft and variation 13° W (FAA NASR via AirNav, effective 29 Oct 2026; both editable in the app).

**Features:** wind & runway entry for departure and arrival, shown as crosswind / headwind in knots on the checklists (ATIS / tower winds are magnetic; a METAR wind is true and is converted with the magnetic variation); takeoff distance over 50 ft for the briefing, with a warning when it exceeds the runway available; Hobbs / tach times (recorded at Inside Cockpit, shown start→end at shutdown); a scan reminder and stage-of-flight EMERGENCY button on emergency checklists.

**Preflight Planning:** weight & balance (stations from `aircraft.js`, fuel, taxi allowance, flight-time burn at the selected cruise power; kept from flight to flight until cleared, a blank entry counts as 0) with limits and a CG envelope plot; then the AFM Section 5 performance (takeoff ground run and over 50 ft, climb, landing ground run and over 50 ft) for the entered temperature, pressure altitude, weight and headwind (knots; gusts not credited, headwind rounded down, tailwind up). Distances are rounded up and climb rates down; nothing is extrapolated toward the unsafe side. Graphs are traced in `tools/charts/*.json` and read by `perf.js`; tables go in as interpolation tables. `node tools/build_charts.js` refuses a chart without the AFM's printed example and sets a safe-side correction factor where the trace doesn't reproduce it. (`perf.js` still labels speeds in MPH from the Seneca charts: switch its labels to knots with the first DA40 chart.)

**Checklist text format** (Edit › a checklist): `Item ... RESPONSE`, `# Section`, `! Caution`, `* highlighted`, `^ pulsing`, `| table | row |`, flight-data tokens (`{dep.sum}`, `{arr.sum}`, `{dep.hw}`, `{tora}`, `{tob.todist}`, `{times.out}`, `{times.in}`), `-> LABEL | subtitle : Checklist title` buttons, and `@EMERGENCY`, `@HIDDEN`, `@BRANCH`, `@ATIS DEP` / `@ATIS ARR`, `@SUBTITLE` directives.

**Updating:** edit `defaults.js` (bump `DEFAULTS_VERSION` and add the previous version's signature to `KNOWN_SIGS` in `index.html`), then bump `APP_VERSION` in `index.html` and `VERSION` in `sw.js`; add any new file (and every chart scan) to `FILES` in `sw.js` and `OFFLINE_FILES` in `index.html`. Edited checklists on the iPad are never overwritten; a change that must reach them goes in as a one-time migration.
